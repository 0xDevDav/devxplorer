# DevXplorer privacy policy

DevXplorer is a file manager that works on your own computer. It does not collect, store or send any personal data, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

## What stays on your computer

- The settings you choose (language, theme, extensions, view) and the statuses and tags you give to files are saved in the app's data folder on your computer.
- Unexpected errors are written to a log file in the same folder, so you can attach it to a bug report if you want to. It is never sent anywhere by the app.

## When DevXplorer connects to the internet

- **Updates (only the version downloaded from GitHub):** the app asks [GitHub](https://github.com/0xDevDav/devxplorer/releases) whether a newer version exists and downloads it. GitHub's [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) applies to that request. The Microsoft Store version is updated by the Store instead.
- **HTML previews:** when you preview a web page saved on your computer, images or styles it links to on the internet are loaded, as in a browser. Scripts in previewed pages never run.
- **Links you open:** the GitHub button in Settings opens the project page in your browser.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on [github.com/0xDevDav/devxplorer](https://github.com/0xDevDav/devxplorer/issues).
